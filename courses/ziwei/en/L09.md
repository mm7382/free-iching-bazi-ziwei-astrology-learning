## In plain words

The Life Palace is about the person themself and is where reading starts; Siblings covers brothers, sisters and peers; Partner covers close relationships; Children covers children, younger people and things you raise yourself. A palace decides the topic, not the answer.

## What the text says

Volume 2 lists the twelve palaces in order from the Life Palace. The text says wife and concubine, and servants; the app shows the Partner Palace and the Friends Palace. Those are modern wordings, not the original terms.
