## In plain words

Read one palace in three steps: say what topic it covers, say which major stars are in it, then join them: this palace covers this topic and holds these stars. Up to here it is observation, not judgement.

## What the text says

The Tai Wei Fu says a star is read in the position it sits in; the palace order comes from Volume 2.
