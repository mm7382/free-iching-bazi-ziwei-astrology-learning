## In plain words

The fourteen major stars (Zi Wei, Tian Ji, Tai Yang and the rest) are the main figures when reading a palace. Finding the stars of your Life Palace means looking at which of them are listed in that cell. There may be one, two, or none. None is called having no major star; it is a common feature of a chart, not missing data and not a mistake.

## What the text says

Volume 3 has a line that begins: if the Life Palace has no major star. That the text discusses the case at all shows it is an ordinary part of charts.

## Compare with your own chart

Write your first observation, facts only: my Life Palace is at such-and-such branch, and the chart lists these stars. If there are none, write that no major star is listed. Do not add one yourself.

## An everyday example

A friend identifies with one major star, but your Life palace looks different. It may contain one, two or no major stars. Read the actual configuration before choosing a star to identify with.

Record the actual number and names of major stars; state when there are none.
