## In plain words

The year layer is about this year. Whatever this year's branch is, the palace with the same branch is the year's Life Palace. 2026 is a Bing Wu year, so the year's Life Palace is the palace at Wu. It is the same rule for everyone, whatever their birth data.

## What the text says

The text calls the year Tai Sui. Volume 3 says that for Tai Sui one looks at its trines and opposite: the year layer is also read with its four positions.
