## In plain words

The month and the year are separate layers: the year changes yearly, the month monthly. Always say which layer you mean, for example: this month's Life Palace is my Wealth Palace; do not mix it up with the year.

## What the text says

The months are counted from Dou Jun, placed by Volume 2's rule on Dou Jun.

## Compare with your own chart

Say which palace is this month's Life Palace, then which is this year's, as two separate sentences.
