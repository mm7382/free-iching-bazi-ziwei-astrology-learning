## In plain words

The text describes the three this way: Zi Wei belongs to Earth and is called the emperor's seat in the central sky; Tian Fu belongs to Earth and is the first commanding star of the Southern Dipper; Tian Xiang belongs to Water, is the fifth star of the Southern Dipper, and is in charge of ranks. These are the text's classifications and offices for the stars, not judgements of character, and say nothing about what happens to whoever has them.

## What the text says

The original lines come from Volume 1, the questions and answers on the stars: one line per star, giving its element, its dipper and its office. Open the classical basis below to read them.

## Zi Wei (Emperor Star) · Direction and responsibility

Direction and responsibility. Friends want different things from a trip. Ask what matters most to each person and agree on shared priorities, instead of planning everything alone.

For one responsibility, name a decision you need to make and one you can share.

## Tian Fu (Treasury Star) · Stewardship and continuity

Stewardship and continuity. Keeping many files is not helpful if the final version cannot be found. A clear system makes saved resources usable.

Organise one frequently used folder or routine, keeping what serves a purpose.

## Tian Xiang (Minister Star) · Coordination and fairness

Coordination and fairness. When sharing chores, consider time and effort rather than counting tasks alone. Agree on the criteria before comparing contributions.

Agree on a clear division of responsibility for one shared task.

On the example, Zi Wei and Tian Xiang share the Travel Palace at Chen, and Tian Fu is in the Inner Life Palace at Zi. So the three stars sit in two cells.

## Find it on a chart.

Find which palace Zi Wei falls in on your chart.

- 紫微屬土，乃中天之尊星為帝座，主掌造化樞機 Zi Wei belongs to Earth. It is the honoured star of the central heavens, taken as the emperor's seat, and is said to hold the pivot of creation. Zi Wei Dou Shu Quan Shu (紫微斗數全書), Volume 1, "Questions and Answers on the Stars" (諸星問答論); traditionally attributed in received editions

- 天府屬土，南斗主令第一星也。為財帛之主宰 Tian Fu belongs to Earth. It is the first commanding star of the Southern Dipper, and is named master of wealth. Zi Wei Dou Shu Quan Shu (紫微斗數全書), Volume 1, "Questions and Answers on the Stars" (諸星問答論); traditionally attributed in received editions

- 天相屬水，南斗第五星也。為司爵之宿 Tian Xiang belongs to Water and is the fifth star of the Southern Dipper. It is called the asterism that administers rank. Zi Wei Dou Shu Quan Shu (紫微斗數全書), Volume 1, "Questions and Answers on the Stars" (諸星問答論); traditionally attributed in received editions
