## In plain words

Decades divide a life into ten-year spans, each tied to one palace. The first begins at the phase's number, and every ten years moves to the next palace. Men born in a yang year and women born in a yin year go forward; men born in a yin year and women born in a yang year go backward.

## What the text says

Volume 2's rule on decades says yang men and yin women start one palace ahead of the Life Palace and go forward. The direction matches modern practice, but the starting palace does not: this app follows the common modern method and starts at the Life Palace. It is a difference between schools, and the screen says so.
