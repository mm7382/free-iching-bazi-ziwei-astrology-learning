## In plain words

Find the year's Life Palace, then from it count the year's Partner, Career and Wealth palaces in the usual order. Each falls on one of your natal palaces, and you read that palace's stars. This is a structural reading, not good or bad.

## What the text says

Volume 3: for Tai Sui, look at its trines and opposite; the year is read with the same relations between palaces as the natal chart.
