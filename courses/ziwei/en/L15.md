## In plain words

Fourteen major stars go into twelve palaces, so some palaces hold two; the text also calls this sharing a degree. When reading, name both stars rather than picking one; this app does not judge which matters more.

## What the text says

Volume 2 has a line on Wu Qu sharing a degree with Lian Zhen. The passage is about the five elements; here only the term is borrowed, not the reasoning.
