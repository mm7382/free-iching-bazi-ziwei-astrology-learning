> My friend and I both have Po Jun. Why do people read it differently for us?

## In plain words

Reading a palace means saying two things: which palace it is (which part of life it is about), and which star is in it (how the text describes that star). Move the same star to another palace and the topic changes; the star's own description does not.

## What the text says

The Tai Wei Fu: the stars share a path and the method has its divisions; the same star is read back in the position it sits in.

## Compare with your own chart

Find which palace Po Jun is in on your chart and compare with the example, where it is in the Life Palace: same star, different palace, different topic.

## An everyday example

Use Tian Tong's theme of ease and connection. In the Partner palace, ask how needs are expressed; in Career, ask about work rhythm. The cue is similar but the life question changes.

Identify the palace's life topic before applying the star's reading cue.

On the example Po Jun is in the Life Palace, so it is read under the topic of the person themself. If your Po Jun is in the Partner Palace, the same star is read under close relationships.
