## In plain words

Take one palace and compare your chart with the example. The palace is the same, so the topic is the same; the stars differ, so the text's focus differs. It is enough to say same topic, different stars; there is no need to say which is better.

## What the text says

The Tai Wei Fu: a star is read in its position, so the same position means the same topic.

## Compare with your own chart

Say which major stars are in your Life Palace, then compare with the example's, which holds Po Jun.
