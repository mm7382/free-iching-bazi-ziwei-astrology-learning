## In plain words

A palace without a major star is called empty. There are two common readings: say plainly that no major star is listed, or, in some schools, look at the opposite palace's stars for reference. This app shows the opposite stars but does not move them in, and does not choose a reading for you.

## What the text says

Volume 3 writes of the case where the Life Palace has no major star: the text already discusses empty palaces.

## Compare with your own chart

Find your empty palaces and look at the stars opposite each. Say them separately: this palace has no major star; its opposite holds such-and-such.

## An everyday example

No major star in Partner does not mean no relationship life. Check supporting stars, then compare the opposite palace, noting that schools differ in how they use borrowed-star readings.

Confirm the absence of major stars, then state the opposite-palace method used.

On the example, Siblings at You has no major star and its opposite, Friends at Mao, holds Tian Ji and Ju Men; Children at Wei is empty and its opposite, Property at Chou, holds Tai Yang and Tai Yin.
