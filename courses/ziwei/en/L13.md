## In plain words

The twelve palaces form a ring. Count six places from any palace and you reach the far side: that is its opposite. The Life Palace is always opposite Travel; Partner is opposite Career, and Wealth is opposite Inner Life.

## What the text says

The Gu Sui Fu speaks of telling apart the roles of a palace and its opposite: reading one palace also means looking across at the other.

## Compare with your own chart

Find your Life Palace, look at the cell across the centre, and confirm it is Travel.
