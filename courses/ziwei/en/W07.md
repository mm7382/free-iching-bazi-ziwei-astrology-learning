> Are the four positions special to my chart, or the same on every chart?

## In plain words

The same counting on a different chart lands on the same names: the Life Palace's four positions are always Life, Travel, Wealth and Career, because they come from the fixed order of the branches. Only the stars inside differ.

## What the text says

Volume 3's order, body and life first, then wealth, career and travel, is followed on both charts.

## Compare with your own chart

Trace it on your chart, then on the example, writing down the four names and their stars both times.

## An everyday example

Choose a current question, such as balancing work and rest. For each of the four positions, record a fact before an interpretation, rather than starting with the answer you want.

Record one fact in each palace before your interpretation.

The example: Life at Xu (Po Jun), Travel at Chen (Zi Wei, Tian Xiang), Wealth at Wu (Qi Sha), Career at Yin (Tan Lang).
