## In plain words

The four positions are the palace itself, its opposite six places away, and the two trines four and eight places away. For the Life Palace they are Life, Travel, Wealth and Career. This follows from the fixed order of the branches and is the same on every chart.

## What the text says

Volume 3 sets the order: first the body and life, then wealth, career and travel, which are exactly the four positions of the Life Palace.

## Compare with your own chart

Count the four positions from your Life Palace, confirm each name, then read out the major stars in each. This lesson is recognition only.

## An everyday example

A job change involves your approach, surroundings, responsibilities and resources. Life, Travel, Career and Wealth can organise these different questions rather than one star answering them all.

Turn Life, Travel, Career and Wealth into four distinct life questions.
