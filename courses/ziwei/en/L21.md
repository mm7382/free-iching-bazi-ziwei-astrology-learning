## In plain words

Before reading a whole chart, check three details: birth date, hour and gender. One hour off can move the Life Palace by a cell. Then read in this order: Life and Body palaces, the phase, the four positions, the other palaces, then the time layers. Each step states chart facts only.

## What the text says

Volume 3 sets out what to look at first and what next, starting from the body and life.

## Compare with your own chart
