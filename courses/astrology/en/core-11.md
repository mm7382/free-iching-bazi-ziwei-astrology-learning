THE IDEA

Houses five to eight: what you put out, what you look after daily, the person opposite you, and what you share with others.

- ASC Houses run in order from the starting point at birth; this lesson looks at the fifth through the eighth. Diagram: a double ring. The outer ring is divided into twelve parts for the signs, the inner ring stands for the houses, and a horizontal line on the left is labelled ASC, where the houses are counted from. A concept figure with no real degrees. 01House five is what you put out; house six is what you look after daily. Houses five and six Astrology today often reads house five as what you make and are willing to show, and house six as what you keep up every day — work habits and the state of your body. House five is also often read as play and delight — anything that makes you feel “I made this, and I love it”; house six is the small unseen things that keep the days holding up. Later interpretation Ask yourself: what have you made lately that you really wanted to show someone? On an ordinary workday, how do you arrange getting up, eating and working? Which small habit, once broken, throws the whole day off? How your body is doing is for a doctor and your own sense to judge. Cited source 02House seven is the person opposite you; house eight is what you share. Houses seven and eight Astrology today often reads house seven as how you deal with an equal — a partner, someone you work with, and an opponent too — and house eight as what you hold jointly with others and what has to end completely before you can move on. House seven sits directly opposite house one on the chart: house one is you, house seven the person standing across from you; house eight is where two people come to share money, responsibility and trust. Later interpretation Ask yourself: with a partner or someone you work with, do you tend to give way or hold firm? Who do you share an account, a home, a duty or a secret with? When a relationship comes to its end, do you cut it off cleanly or let go slowly? Reading the four together An easy order to remember: house five is what you put out, house six what you keep up daily, house seven is an equal standing opposite you, and house eight is what the two of you come to share. Follow the line with questions: is anyone receiving what I put out? Do my daily habits leave room for the people close to me? Later interpretation In many charts these four houses are empty. An empty house simply has no body in it; partners, work and shared property can still be read from these houses. LOOK CLOSER

## A common mix-up

House eight looks at what you share and carry together with others, and how a chapter closes. It describes how you meet these things, such as how you split money and responsibility with someone.

An empty house only means no body sits there; ten bodies across twelve houses always leaves some empty.

House six is good for looking at how you keep up your daily routine — whether you eat and sleep at set times or forget everything once you are busy. If you are unwell, see a doctor.

## Now, try it yourself.

- **William Lilly, Christian Astrology (1647)**“Of the Twelve Houses” gives: the fifth house children, ambassadors, the state of a woman with child, banquets, taverns, plays and the father's wealth; the sixth servants, lesser cattle and the profit or loss from them, sickness with its quality and cause, whether curable, whether short or long, and day labourers; the seventh marriage, the description of the person asked about, love questions, public enemies, the defendant, the opposing party in war, quarrels and duels; the eighth the estate of the deceased, death with its quality and nature, wills and legacies, and the wife's dowry. Reading them as making and showing, daily upkeep, equal relationships, and sharing and ending are later summaries; the servants, slaves, cattle, medical diagnosis and dowry framing are not carried over, as the lesson states.
