THE IDEA

Your task: read one transit on the today page and judge how long it lasts.

Table: the Moon stays in a sign about two days, the Sun about a month, Mars about two months, Jupiter about a year and Saturn about two and a half years. All averages from NASA orbit periods; retrograde motion changes any single pass.

A transit says which moving body has reached which of your placements.

## What one transit line says

A transit has three parts: the body that is moving, the placement of yours it has reached, and the angle between them. Pull those three apart before you think about meaning; in that order, what you see is a clear relationship between positions.

This app works out the day's positions once a day and compares them with your positions at birth. The allowed gap is 1 degree in general and 2 for the Sun and Moon; within 0.1 of a degree counts as “exact.”

What the old texts say: the timing chapter of Ptolemy's Tetrabiblos is Book IV ch. 10, the division of times, which splits a life into stretches by rule and reads each stretch in turn.

To know how long it lasts, look first at which body is moving.

## How long it lasts

The same angle can last very different lengths of time depending on which body is moving. The Moon is past in a day; the Sun in about two; Mars within 1 degree usually a few days; Jupiter a week or two; Saturn and the planets further out can come and go over months, because retrograde motion brings them back. These are rough figures worked out from NASA orbit periods.

So when you see a transit, first ask who is moving. If it is the Moon, it is today's matter at most.

If Saturn is the one moving, it is a theme you have been meeting again and again lately, one that stays for quite a while. Ask yourself: which matter has kept coming back to me these past months?

What the old texts say: Book I ch. 2 of the Tetrabiblos says predictions sometimes fail, and that more than the heavens shapes a person.

## A common mix-up

The today page describes two sets of degrees — the day's and your birth chart's — forming an angle. You can check it against how your day went.

The positions really are calculated, but Ptolemy himself says predictions fail and that more than the sky shapes a person. Feeling nothing is a normal result.
