THE IDEA

Your task: read your Moon, then check it against one real moment of pressure.

Table: the Moon stays in a sign about two days, the Sun about a month, Mars about two months, Jupiter about a year and Saturn about two and a half years. All averages from NASA orbit periods; retrograde motion changes any single pass.

The Moon changes sign every couple of days, so it often has two candidates — read both.

## Read the candidates together

The Moon goes round the Earth in about 27.32 days (NASA figure), which works out at a new sign every couple of days. So people without a birth time very often get two candidates.

This time, read both, then see which one is closer to how you actually react.

The comparison becomes one observation of your own, ready for your note. Both candidates stay side by side in the chart, so you can check them again whenever something new happens.

What the old texts say: Book III ch. 13 of Ptolemy's Tetrabiblos gives the feeling, non-rational part of a person to the Moon and the rational part to Mercury, and says such judgements need many observations together.

Recall how you really reacted first, then read the description.

## Check it against one real moment

The check starts with something that really happened: recall it first, then compare. Think of the last time you really felt under pressure: was your first move to speak up, go quiet, leave, or busy yourself with something else? Write it down, then see whether the Moon's description matches.

A mismatch is a result too. Maybe you read the wrong candidate, or maybe that reaction comes more from how you were raised than from the chart. Both are honest, and both belong in your note.

What the old texts say: Book I ch. 2 of the Tetrabiblos says predictions sometimes fail, and that seed, place of birth, upbringing and custom all make a difference and must be considered too.

Teaching example

## Demonstration, not a real person

Candidate one, Moon in Aries: under pressure, act and speak first.

Candidate two, Moon in Taurus: under pressure, stop and steady yourself first.

Think of the last time plans changed at short notice: did you phone at once, or sit down and replan?

The point is that both candidates are listed, so you read and compare both. · This is a teaching example, not your chart.

OBSERVE YOUR OWN CHART

## Look at my chart

Your turn: write your reaction first, then read the description — not the other way round.

- First write down your first move the last time you were under pressure.

- Then read your Moon sign. If there are two candidates, read both.

- Write one line: what matched, and what did not?

Without a birth time, rising sign and houses stay blank.
