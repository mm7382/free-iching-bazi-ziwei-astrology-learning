THE IDEA

Houses nine to twelve: what you reach for, how you are seen, the people with you, and where you trip yourself up.

- ASC The twelve houses run once around from the starting point on the left; this lesson looks at the last four. Diagram: a double ring. The outer ring is divided into twelve parts for the signs, the inner ring stands for the houses, and a horizontal line on the left is labelled ASC, where the houses are counted from. A concept figure with no real degrees. 01House nine is what you reach out for; house ten is how the world sees you. Houses nine and ten Astrology today often reads house nine as how far you will go to understand something — study, going abroad, what you believe — and house ten as how you are known in public and the direction of your work. House nine is like the edge of your map: the books you have read, the places you have been and the ideas you hold all push that map outward. Later interpretation House ten sits at the top of the chart, its most visible spot. It looks at public standing and the direction of your work: how people out there introduce you, where your work is heading, and what you want to be remembered for. Later interpretation Ask yourself: is there something lately you badly want to read about, ask about or go and see? If a colleague or client summed you up in one line, what would they say — and is that what you want? Reflection prompt What the old texts say: Lilly's “Of the Twelve Houses” gives house nine long voyages over the sea, religious people and clergy of every kind, dreams and visions, foreign countries, books and learning; house ten kings, princes, judges and everyone in authority, mothers, honour, promotion, office, and the profession or trade a person follows. Cited source 02House eleven is the people with you; house twelve is how you trip yourself up. Houses eleven and twelve Astrology today often reads house eleven as the people with you and the groups you want to join, and house twelve as the side of yourself you would rather not look at and what you can only deal with alone. The most useful reading of house twelve is “self-undoing”: what blocks you is usually not an outside enemy but your own habits. House eleven looks at the people around you and the hopes you hold for the future. Later interpretation Ask yourself: where do you tend to hold yourself back — putting things off, saying yes and not following through, refusing to stop when you are worn out? What only becomes clear when you are quiet and alone? Is there a group around you where you feel you belong? Reflection prompt What the old texts say: Lilly gives house eleven, by nature, friends and friendship, hope, trust, the praise or blame a person gets, and whether friends are faithful; house twelve private enemies, witches, sorrow, trouble, imprisonment, every kind of affliction, self-undoing, and those who secretly scheme against their neighbours. LOOK CLOSER

## A common mix-up

The most useful reading of house twelve is self-undoing: where you hold yourself back and what only becomes clear alone. It describes habits, not fate.

House ten looks at public standing and the direction of your work: how people out there know you and where your work is heading. Home and the people who raised you are read in house four.

A house is read from its bodies and its ruler together, as a whole; an empty house still has its subject — it is just quiet in the chart.

## Now, try it yourself.

- **William Lilly, Christian Astrology (1647)**“Of the Twelve Houses” gives: the ninth house long voyages beyond seas, religious men and clergy, dreams and visions, foreign countries, books and learning; the tenth kings and princes, judges and officers in authority, mothers, honour, preferment, office and the profession followed; the eleventh friends and friendship, hope, trust, praise or dispraise and the faithfulness of friends; the twelfth private enemies, witches, sorrow, tribulation, imprisonment, affliction and self-undoing. Reading them as how far you go to understand, public standing and work, company and groups, and what is dealt with alone are later summaries; the assignment of mothers, and the witches and imprisonment material, are not carried over, as the lesson states.
