THE IDEA

Task: set up your own chart and find out what each field changes.

- ASC The time you enter sets the starting point on the left, and with it the twelve houses on the inner ring. Diagram: a double ring. The outer ring is divided into twelve parts for the signs, the inner ring stands for the houses, and a horizontal line on the left is labelled ASC, the starting point. A concept figure with no real degrees. 01Date, time, place and time zone each change your chart. What the four fields do The date fixes which sign the Sun and most planets are in. The time fixes the Ascendant, the Midheaven and the twelve houses; fill it in and that layer appears. The place plus the time zone fix what the clock said where you were at that moment. How this tool works One time zone out moves the whole chart an hour earlier or later. So a wrong time zone matters as much as a wrong birth time. Derived calculation This app’s city search brings the time zone with it, which is safer than typing one in yourself. How this tool works What the old texts say: in Book III ch. 2 of the Tetrabiblos, Ptolemy takes the degree rising on the eastern horizon at the moment of birth as the chart’s starting point, so both the moment and the place are needed. Cited source 02Once the chart is set up, check your typing, the blanks and the candidates, then start reading. Three checks once it is set up Once the chart exists, check three things. First, go back over the date, time and place you typed. Second, look at the Ascendant row. Third, see whether any body is marked “unconfirmed” with two candidates. Reflection prompt If you entered no time, the Ascendant row stays empty, which is exactly the correct result. A body marked “unconfirmed” changed sign on the day you were born. You can edit your data in settings at any time, and the whole chart is worked out again. How this tool works The two most common typing slips are mixing up morning and afternoon (2:30 and 14:30 are twelve hours apart) and swapping month and day (4 March entered as 3 April). With those three checked, your chart is solid material to work with. Reflection prompt Teaching example Demonstration data Enter 1990-03-21, Taipei, time unknown: the Sun is Pisces or Aries, two candidates. Add 14:30 to the same day: the Sun settles on one sign, and the Ascendant and houses appear. Set the time zone to Tokyo by mistake and the chart moves an hour; the Ascendant may change sign. Fixed demonstration data: same day, same place, changing only the time or the time zone shows what each field does. · This is a teaching example, not your chart. LOOK CLOSER

## A common mix-up

One zone out is an hour out. The Ascendant changes sign roughly every two hours, and the whole set of houses moves with it.

With no birth time, empty is the correct result; once you add a time, the Ascendant and houses appear.
