A line built this way has real content and says more than “I have a Mercury–Saturn square.” Reflection prompt For example: “Mercury is how I think and talk, Saturn is how I set limits and demands on myself; they are square, so I often check myself before I speak.” A common reading: a square is like two forces pulling against each other, a trine or sextile like two forces running the same way; a smooth relation is easy to use, while one that needs coordinating is often where you put in the most work and grow the most solid. Later interpretation To weigh the relation, ask two things: does it run smoothly, and does it take effort to coordinate? Those two questions let you describe the relation concretely. LOOK CLOSER

## A common mix-up

How many aspects you get depends on the orbs used; the count is simply what the settings produce. Each aspect describes a relation between two bodies, and the relation itself is what is worth looking at.

Tight means close to exact. The old text judges from many conditions taken together.
