THE IDEA

Your task: compare two charts in one order, and write down only where they differ.

Diagram: an outer ring labelled Sun, an inner ring labelled Moon, and a point at the bottom labelled Ascendant, stacked on one vertical line to show three different layers rather than real degrees.

Compare one layer at a time, never all mixed together.

## The order to compare in

Compare two charts in the same order you read your own: Sun first, then Moon, then Ascendant. For each layer write only “same” or where they differ, and save any conclusion until all three layers are done.

The Sun is the direction a person faces outward, the Moon is how they settle themselves, and the Ascendant is how they walk into a room. When you compare, turn the three layers into three questions: where are they heading? How do they settle themselves? How do they walk into a new place?

Compare Ascendants only when both people have an exact birth time. If either one is missing it, skip straight past this layer and compare the Sun and Moon, so the whole comparison rests on solid data.

What the old texts say: Book III ch. 13 of Ptolemy's Tetrabiblos says the impulses of the soul vary a great deal, so they need many observations together, never a simple or offhand judgement.

Comparing two charts is for saying clearly how two people differ.

## Compare differences, not scores

When you finish comparing two charts, you hold a list of differences: Sun, Moon and Ascendant, one line each on where the two people are the same and where they differ. A relationship is something two real people live out together, and this list helps you understand each other and start talking more easily.

The useful part of comparing two charts is that it helps you say something specific, like “the way I settle is not the way they settle.” Being able to say the difference is worth far more than a score.

What the old texts say: Book IV ch. 5 of the Tetrabiblos is the marriage chapter, and it opens by looking at the Moon in men's charts and the Sun in wives'.

LOOK CLOSER

## A common mix-up

Comparing charts is for saying the difference clearly, such as how each person settles themselves; how well two people get on shows in their life together.

The Ascendant changes sign about every two hours on average, so it needs an exact time to be right. Without one, just skip that layer.
