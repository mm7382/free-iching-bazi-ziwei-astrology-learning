The first stem in each row is the main one. Start with just that one, especially in the day branch.

## What hidden stems are

Each branch holds one to three stems. Yin holds Jia Wood, Bing Fire and Wu Earth; Zi holds only Gui Water.

Think of stems as what is said aloud and hidden stems as what runs underneath.

## Why it matters

Stems alone make a person look flat. Hidden stems show someone doing one thing on the surface while wanting another underneath.

Direct Officer on the surface with Hurting Officer hidden: follows the rules, constantly wants out.

## How to use it as a beginner

Start with each branch’s main hidden stem. The Day branch’s main stem matters most — it sits closest to you.

Leave the rest for later.

> Stems are spoken, hidden stems run underneath. Start with the main one.
