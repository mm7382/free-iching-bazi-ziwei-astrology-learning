First the direction, then whether yin and yang match.

## How to read your first relationship

Take the Month Pillar stem, right next to your Day Master, and compare the two. Ask just two things: how their phases relate, and whether they're both yin or both yang.

There are only five possibilities: same as me, what I produce, what I control, what controls me, and what nourishes me. Which one it is sets the basic tone.

Say your Day Master is Xin Metal and the month stem is Ren Water. Metal generates Water, so Ren falls under what I produce. That stands for your output and self-expression.

## Put it into one everyday sentence

Lots of what I produce means you tend to pour energy into output: making things, talking, teaching, raising kids. You leave something behind, but it can wear you out.

Lots of what controls me means outside demands keep pushing you: duties, roles, expectations. You get structure, but little room to breathe.

Lots of what nourishes me means you have support and knowledge to lean on. You feel steady, but your drive to act can get dulled.

## How to practice

Don't try to read all eight characters at once. Each day, compare just one to your Day Master, sum it up in one everyday sentence, and stop there.

The Today tab gives you one question a day built on exactly this: the day’s stem compared with your Day Master. It's the easiest place to practice.

Keep it up for two weeks and you'll be able to say it without looking anything up. Then the Ten Gods names will click fast.

> Read one relationship at a time and put it into one everyday sentence. That habit does more for you than memorizing the whole Ten Gods table.
