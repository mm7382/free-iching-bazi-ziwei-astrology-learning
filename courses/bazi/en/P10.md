## The five groups

Companions = peers and competition. Output = what you make and express. Wealth = resources you command.

Officers = duty and pressure. Resource = support and learning. Those five cover most of a life.

## Too much, too little

Many companions: partners and rivals, head-on friction. Few: self-reliant but alone. Much output: expressive, draining, friction with rules.

Much wealth: chances but distraction. Much officer: duty and no breathing room. Much resource: steady but passive. Each has two faces.

## Applying it

Count which group is most and least present. The largest is your default; the smallest is usually what to practise.

Much resource and little output: reads a lot, makes little. The practice is making, not reading more.

> Five groups suffice: peers, output, resources, duty, support. The largest is your default; the smallest is your homework.
