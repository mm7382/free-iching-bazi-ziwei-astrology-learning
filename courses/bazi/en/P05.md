Ten stems are five forces split by polarity — two temperaments per force.

## What polarity changes

Yang goes outward, direct, in large amounts. Yin goes inward, fine, sustained. Neither is stronger.

Jia Wood is a tree pushing straight up; Yi Wood is a vine that goes around. Both are Wood.

## The ten stems at a glance

Jia/Yi Wood, Bing/Ding Fire, Wu/Ji Earth, Geng/Xin Metal, Ren/Gui Water — yang first, yin second.

Bing is sunlight: far-reaching. Ding is candlelight: close and lasting. Geng is an axe, Xin a blade.

## Applying it

Check whether your Day Master is yang or yin. Yang tends to act then adjust; yin tends to settle then act.

Not better or worse — a default to recognise, so you know which side to practise.

> Polarity is temperament, not strength. Start by identifying your own.

#### What is the usual default for a yang Day Master?

Yang acts then adjusts; yin settles then acts. Knowing your default tells you what to practise.
