Six Harmonies pair branches. Things in that area run smoother, and pull you along.

Original app lesson · freely available here

A pair is a place to start looking. It does not predict events on its own.

## The six pairs

Zi–Chou, Yin–Hai, Mao–Xu, Chen–You, Si–Shen, Wu–Wei.

Harmony is not sameness; it is fit. Two different people who get along.

## What it feels like

Where there is harmony, things feel effortless: easy conversations, work that connects by itself.

The cost is attachment. People stay where it is easy, and it becomes a comfort zone.

## How the Today tab uses it

The Today tab compares the day branch with your birth-year branch and adds a line when they harmonise.

It describes fit for that day. It is not a prediction and carries no score.

> Harmony means fit, not fortune. Easy places become places you will not leave.
