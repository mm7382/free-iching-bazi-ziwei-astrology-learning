A Six Clash means two branches collide. Collision brings movement — and movement brings change.

Original app lesson · freely available here

A pair is a place to start looking. It does not predict events on its own.

## The six clashes

Zi–Wu, Chou–Wei, Yin–Shen, Mao–You, Chen–Xu, Si–Hai.

A clash is two opposing forces meeting. That area will not stay still.

## A clash is not necessarily bad

A clash means movement: moving house, changing jobs, shifting relationships. For someone stuck, that is an opening.

But details slip while things move, so leave slack and do not overbook.

## Using it day to day

The Today tab adds a line when the day branch clashes with your birth-year branch. Not a warning — a note that the rhythm breaks easily.

In practice: put important work where it will not be interrupted, and check once more before sending.
