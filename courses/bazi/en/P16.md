## What one day changes

The day pillar changes every day, so one day shifts the Day Master — and every Ten God is recomputed.

That is why two people born a day apart can read nothing alike.

## What one hour changes

Only the hour pillar moves; the Day Master holds. The outline stays, and later life, children and the private self shift.

That is why an uncertain hour is not a disaster.

## What a wrong time zone changes

A wrong time zone can push the time past midnight or a solar term, changing the day or even month pillar. This is the worst error.

Getting the birthplace right matters more than any interpretive skill.

> A wrong hour moves one pillar; a wrong time zone can move everything. Fix the time zone first.
