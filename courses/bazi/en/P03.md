Check three things first: the time zone, solar or lunar calendar, and whether the time is really known.

## Where the four pillars come from

The year pillar follows the term-based year, the month pillar the solar term, the day pillar a continuous sexagenary count, the hour pillar the hour combined with the day stem.

All four are lookup and arithmetic, so apps should agree. Differences usually come from time zone or term handling.

## The three things worth checking

One: the birthplace time zone. Two: solar versus lunar date. Three: whether the hour is known or guessed.

Get those right and you can trust the output.

## Then comes the real work

Calculation just lays out the data. The work is comparing each cell to the Day Master and saying it plainly.

The rest of the course does that and does not return to arithmetic.
