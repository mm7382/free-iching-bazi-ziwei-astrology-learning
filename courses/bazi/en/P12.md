Stem combinations tie two characters together, and tied force does not act as before.

Original app lesson · freely available here

## Which stems combine

Jia–Ji, Yi–Geng, Bing–Xin, Ding–Ren, Wu–Gui. Think of two people holding hands.

Neither acts as freely alone; each holds part of the other’s attention.

## What it means for a person

A combination often shows as being automatically drawn to a type of person or situation. It can be support or entanglement.

If your Day Master combines with something, that area is hard to stay detached from.

## Do not over-read it

A combination is not luck, marriage or money. It only says two forces hold each other.

As a beginner, note “I lose detachment here” and move on.

> A combination ties, it does not bless. Note where you lose detachment.
