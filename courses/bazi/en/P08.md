LESSON P08 / 11 OF 20

From the Day Master outward, each pillar speaks about a different area.

Original app lesson · freely available here

The closer a pillar is to the Day Pillar, the more personal it gets.

## What each pillar covers

Year: where you come from. Month: home ground — upbringing, how you work, where you are strongest.

Day: yourself and your closest bond (the Day branch is often read for a partner). Hour: later life, children, the private self.

## Distance means closeness

The closer a pillar sits to the Day Pillar, the more immediate its effect. Month and Day branch are felt most.

So start with the Day branch and the month pillar.

## A short full practice

Compare the Day branch’s main stem to your Day Master and say one plain sentence: often your pattern in close relationships.

Do the same with the month stem for your pattern at work.

> Read the Day branch and month pillar first. The year is background, the hour is direction.
