Remain capable without complacency, judge appropriate limits, and fulfill responsibilities with humility

## Judgment and Plain-Language Notes

Qian: Success. The noble person brings things to completion.

Commentary on the Judgment: “Modesty brings success. Heaven's way descends to aid and shines brightly; Earth's way is lowly yet rises. Heaven diminishes fullness and adds to modesty; Earth transforms fullness and flows toward modesty. Spirits harm fullness and bless modesty; people dislike fullness and favor modesty. Modesty is honored and radiant, lowly yet unsurpassable: this is the noble person's fulfillment.” Commentary on the Image: “A mountain within the Earth: Modesty. The noble person reduces excess and supplements scarcity, weighing things and distributing fairly.” (Zhouyi, Commentaries on the Judgment and the Image; public domain)

Modesty has Kun above and Gen below, a mountain hidden within Earth, symbolizing strength without display. “Success” and “the noble person brings things to completion” affirm humility, while the Image calls for reducing excess, filling shortages, and measured distribution. Modesty means acknowledging limits and respecting others, rather than denying yourself or always yielding. When necessary, it still clearly upholds responsibilities and boundaries.

“Success; the noble person brings things to completion,” along with several lines' good fortune and “nothing unfavorable,” gives humility and proper restraint a positive direction, hence the rating: Highly Favorable. The fifth and top sixes still require corrective action. Modesty cannot mean yielding in everything or guaranteeing outcomes.

Do: Assess yourself honestly and assign credit and responsibility appropriately. Avoid: Deliberate self-deprecation to win approval or ceasing to listen after success.

## Understand This Hexagram Through Its Structure

Kun above, Gen below: the only solid line is the third, with the entire Kun trigram above it.

- Identify Modesty's upper and lower trigrams.

- Find the position of the hexagram's only solid line.

- Explain what the image of a mountain beneath the ground means.

Modesty has Gen below, with two broken lower lines and a solid top line. Kun above has three broken lines. Only the third line in the whole hexagram is solid. Gen is mountain and Kun is Earth, giving the image of a mountain beneath the ground.

A mountain normally rises above the ground, but this hexagram puts it below. The single solid line has real substance, yet it stands at the lower trigram's top rather than at the very top. Structurally, this means possessing strength without placing yourself above others: having substance while taking a low position. This is strength placed unobtrusively, rather than weakness.

Teaching example, not a prediction: The most technically skilled person chooses no title and steps in only when needed. Modesty describes this structure; it does not claim this approach is always better.

## The Six Lines: Original Text and Reflection

### Six at the beginning: A modest, modest noble person. Crossing the great river brings good fortune.

Commentary on the Image: “The modest, modest noble person nurtures themselves through lowliness.” Even at the beginning, remain humble and willing to acknowledge shortcomings. Preparing and acting in this spirit when facing major matters is the direction this line affirms. (Zhouyi, Commentary on the Image; public domain)

Even at the beginning, remain humble and willing to acknowledge shortcomings. Preparing and acting in this spirit when facing major matters is the direction this line affirms.

When first taking responsibility, ask clear questions and complete your preparation. Make seeking advice part of action instead of pretending to know everything already.

What have you not yet mastered that you can openly ask about rather than conceal?

### Six in the second place: Modesty makes itself heard. Steadfastness brings good fortune.

Commentary on the Image: “Modesty makes itself heard; steadfastness brings good fortune, because it is attained within the heart.” Humility emerges from an inner attitude and is felt by others. Remaining correct concerns sincere consistency rather than deliberately constructing a humble image. (Zhouyi, Commentary on the Image; public domain)

Humility emerges from an inner attitude and is felt by others. Remaining correct concerns sincere consistency rather than deliberately constructing a humble image.

Keep words and actions consistent, listen to feedback, and be willing to revise. These express real humility better than repeated self-deprecation.

Does the humility you show come from real respect, or are you waiting to be praised?

### Nine in the third place: A noble person of achievement and modesty. Completion brings good fortune.

Commentary on the Image: “The noble person of achievement and modesty wins the people's allegiance.” Remain humble despite achievements, avoid claiming all credit, and see things through. This line emphasizes that accomplishment and continued responsibility can coexist. (Zhouyi, Commentary on the Image; public domain)

Remain humble despite achievements, avoid claiming all credit, and see things through. This line emphasizes that accomplishment and continued responsibility can coexist.

After important work, acknowledge your contribution as well as others' effort. Arrange follow-up responsibilities properly rather than stopping at taking credit.

After results appear, are you still willing to do the less visible follow-up work well?

### Six in the fourth place: Nothing unfavorable. Put modesty into action.

Commentary on the Image: “Nothing unfavorable in practicing modesty, because it does not violate the rules.” Express humility in action, advancing and withdrawing within appropriate limits. This means knowing when to assist and when to step aside, rather than merely speaking politely. (Zhouyi, Commentary on the Image; public domain)

Express humility in action, advancing and withdrawing within appropriate limits. This means knowing when to assist and when to step aside, rather than merely speaking politely.

When dividing work, know the limits of your authority and give others room when needed. Do not prove your ability by deciding for them.

Where would it be appropriate to assist rather than take control on someone else's behalf?

### Six in the fifth place: Not wealthy, with one's neighbors. It is beneficial to undertake corrective campaigns. Nothing unfavorable.

Commentary on the Image: “Corrective campaigns are beneficial: they address those who do not submit.” Neighbors are connected by more than wealth. Necessary action may still restore order when others refuse to follow it; humility does not exclude principles or intervention. (Zhouyi, Commentary on the Image; public domain)

Neighbors are connected by more than wealth. Necessary action may still restore order when others refuse to follow it; humility does not exclude principles or intervention.

When boundaries are repeatedly crossed, explain the rules and then take proportionate measures as agreed. Endurance need not be your only choice.

Are you mistaking fear of setting boundaries for the humility you ought to show?

### Six at the top: Modesty makes itself heard. It is beneficial to mobilize the army and bring one's own towns and state to order.

Commentary on the Image: “Modesty makes itself heard, but the purpose is not yet attained. The army may be mobilized against one's own towns and state.” Humility may have a reputation while its aim remains unfulfilled. The text directs action toward one's own domain, reminding us to first put the area we are responsible for in order. (Zhouyi, Commentary on the Image; public domain)

Humility may have a reputation while its aim remains unfulfilled. The text directs action toward one's own domain, reminding us to first put the area we are responsible for in order.

If ideals and reality still differ, first examine your own methods and internal team problems instead of immediately demanding complete cooperation from the outside world.

What specific problem can you first correct within your own area of responsibility?

## Quick Quiz

- The third line

- The sixth line

- The first line

**The third line (Correct)** : Correct. Gen's solid line is at the top of the lower trigram, the third line of the hexagram.

**The sixth line** : A solid sixth line with all others broken forms Bo · Splitting Apart.

**The first line** : A solid first line forms Fu · Return.

- Gen (Mountain) below and Kun (Earth) above

- Kun below and Gen above

- All six lines are yin, so it is Earth

**Gen (Mountain) below and Kun (Earth) above (Correct)** : Correct. Read upward: mountain below, Earth above.

**Kun below and Gen above** : That arrangement is Bo, not Modesty.

**All six lines are yin, so it is Earth** : Six yin lines form Kun. Modesty has one solid line.

- Bo · Splitting Apart

- Yu · Enthusiasm

- Kun · The Receptive

**Bo · Splitting Apart (Correct)** : Correct. Kun below and Gen above form Hexagram 23, Bo. The solid line moves from the third to the sixth position.

**Yu · Enthusiasm** : Yu has Kun below and Zhen above, with a solid fourth line.

**Kun · The Receptive** : Kun has six yin lines and no solid lines. Exchanging positions does not make a solid line disappear.
