Beginner articles

This page says who writes the beginner articles, how they are written, and what you may do with them.

## Who writes these?

The articles are written and maintained by **Wei Chun Chuang**. They cover four subjects — the I Ching, Bazi, Zi Wei Dou Shu and Western astrology — and there are 37 of them, each in Traditional Chinese and English.

The same author builds the matching learning apps and free browser tools: [YiJian](https://mm7382.github.io/yijian/) (I Ching casting and all sixty-four hexagrams, an iPhone app with a free web version), [Shilai – Bazi](https://mm7382.github.io/bazi/), [Shilai – Ziwei](https://mm7382.github.io/ziwei/) and [Shilai – Astrology](https://mm7382.github.io/astrology/). The articles and the tools are two halves of the same material: the articles explain the ideas, the tools let you build a real chart and look at it.

## Editorial principles

The usual failure in this subject is blurring together the classical text, later commentary, and the writer's own opinion. Here they are kept apart and labelled:

- Quotations from classical sources are attributed to the source.

- Later commentary and common readings are marked as interpretation, not presented as the original text.

- The author's own summaries, analogies and conversions are presented as this article's own framing, never attributed to a classical source.

- Outcomes are never stated as inevitable, and no article passes a verdict of good or bad fortune for the reader.

Every article is free to read, with no account and no advertising. Charts built with the browser tools stay in your own browser and are never uploaded.

## What this is not

This is introductory material for learning and reflection. It is not a divination consultancy and it does not offer individual readings. For medical, legal, financial or other major decisions, please consult a qualified professional.
