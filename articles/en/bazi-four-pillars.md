Bazi, often called the Four Pillars of Destiny, records the year, month, day and hour of a person's birth using heavenly stems and earthly branches. Each unit of time is written as one stem-branch pair, giving four pairs and eight characters; "bazi" literally means "eight characters". This article introduces the four pillars, the stems and branches and the Five Phases, so you can follow the basic structure of a chart.

## What are the four pillars?

The four pillars are the year pillar, month pillar, day pillar and hour pillar. Each pillar is one heavenly stem on top and one earthly branch below. For example, if someone's day pillar is Jia-Zi, Jia is the stem and Zi is the branch.

Four pillars of two characters each make eight characters, which is where the name comes from.

## What are the heavenly stems and earthly branches?

There are ten heavenly stems: Jia, Yi, Bing, Ding, Wu, Ji, Geng, Xin, Ren, Gui.

There are twelve earthly branches: Zi, Chou, Yin, Mao, Chen, Si, Wu, Wei, Shen, You, Xu, Hai.

Stems and branches are paired in order, Jia-Zi, Yi-Chou and so on, until Gui-Hai completes sixty pairs, the sexagenary cycle, which then starts again from Jia-Zi. This cycle was used to record years, months, days and hours.

## How do stems and branches map to the Five Phases?

The Five Phases (also called Five Elements) are Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal and Water. Every stem and branch belongs to one phase, and stems also have a polarity:

- Jia (yang) and Yi (yin): Wood

- Bing (yang) and Ding (yin): Fire

- Wu (yang) and Ji (yin): Earth

- Geng (yang) and Xin (yin): Metal

- Ren (yang) and Gui (yin): Water

For branches: Yin and Mao are Wood, Si and Wu are Fire, Shen and You are Metal, Hai and Zi are Water, and Chen, Xu, Chou and Wei are Earth. Branches also contain "hidden stems", which become important in more advanced reading.

## How do the Five Phases relate to each other?

There are two basic relationships. Generation: Wood feeds Fire, Fire makes Earth, Earth bears Metal, Metal carries Water, Water nourishes Wood, a cycle of passing on and nurturing. Control: Wood controls Earth, Earth controls Water, Water controls Fire, Fire controls Metal, Metal controls Wood, a cycle of checks and balances.

Both are neutral descriptions, not good or bad. A chart contains both, and together they form its overall shape.

## How are the four pillars calculated?

The year pillar usually changes at Lichun, the "Start of Spring" solar term, rather than at Lunar New Year. Month pillars change at solar terms too; for example, the period from Lichun to Jingzhe is the Yin month. The day pillar follows the sixty-day cycle, and the hour pillar uses two-hour periods; for example, 11 p.m. to 1 a.m. is the Zi hour.

Because solar terms and time zones are involved, birth time and place both affect the chart, so most people now let a perpetual calendar or software do the calculation.

## Where do you start reading a chart?

- Find the Day Master: the heavenly stem of the day pillar, which represents "you" in the chart.

- Label each of the eight characters with its phase and see which phases are plentiful and which are scarce.

- Notice whether the other stems and branches generate or control the Day Master.

Once these three steps feel familiar, you are ready for topics such as the Ten Gods, combinations and clashes.

## FAQ

**How does Bazi relate to the Chinese zodiac animal?**

Your zodiac animal is the branch of the year pillar, for example Zi is the Rat and Chou is the Ox. The zodiac looks only at the year branch; Bazi looks at all four pillars.

**Why doesn't the year pillar start at Lunar New Year?**

Bazi divides years and months by solar terms, and the year begins at Lichun. People born near Lichun may therefore have a year pillar that differs from the zodiac year they expect.

**Does it matter if the Five Phases are unevenly spread in my chart?**

Eight characters can't cover five phases evenly, so an uneven spread is very common. While learning, treat it as a description of the chart's structure and read it together with the Day Master and the other relationships.
